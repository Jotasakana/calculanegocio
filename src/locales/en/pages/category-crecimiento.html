Two different questions

Break-even looks forward: it answers “how much do I have to sell to avoid losing money?” and is useful before you set a price or take on a fixed expense. ROI looks back, or at a specific investment: it answers “how much have I gained compared with what I put in?”. Neither takes risk into account, and ROI does not take time into account either.

Both calculators in this category work with the figures you type and add no tax. To understand how much of each sale is margin, see the {{category.title:margenes-y-precios}} category.
