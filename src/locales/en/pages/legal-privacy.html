Summary

CalculaNegocio is designed to work without collecting personal data. There are no user registrations, accounts, forms, comments, newsletters or payments, and the site uses no analytics, advertising or cookies (see the cookie policy).

What data is processed

The amounts you enter in the calculators

Calculations run in your browser. The amounts and percentages you type are not sent to any server, are not stored and are not linked to you.

Technical data about your visit

The site is hosted on {{legal:hosting}}. To deliver the pages to you, the hosting provider technically receives your IP address and the request data (requested page, browser type, date and time) and may log them for security, operation and abuse-prevention purposes, under its own privacy policy. This site does not use those logs to identify you and does not build profiles.

Messages you send us

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in handling your enquiry (article 6.1.f GDPR) or, where you ask for something to be done, taking steps at your request (article 6.1.b).

Recipients and international transfers

We do not disclose personal data to third parties, except where required by law. The hosting provider ({{legal:hosting}}) acts as a technical service provider and may process data outside the European Economic Area; in that case it relies on the safeguards it publishes itself.

Retention

Messages are kept for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and, afterwards, for the legal limitation periods for possible liabilities. The hosting provider’s technical logs are kept under its own terms.

Your rights

Security

The site is delivered over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and consists of static pages: it has no user database and no private area.
