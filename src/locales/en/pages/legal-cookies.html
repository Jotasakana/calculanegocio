What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies (such as the browser’s local storage) are small files or pieces of data that a site stores on your device to remember information or recognise you. Those that are not strictly necessary require your prior consent.

What this site uses

CalculaNegocio does not use first-party or third-party cookies, local storage (localStorage, sessionStorage) or similar identifiers. It also loads no scripts, fonts or images from other domains. The calculators work without saving anything on your device, and the language selector does not remember your choice.

For this reason the site shows no cookie banner: there are no cookies to accept or reject.
