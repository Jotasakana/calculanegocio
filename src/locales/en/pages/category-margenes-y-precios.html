Margin and markup are not the same thing

Margin is measured on the selling price and markup is measured on the cost. With a cost of 60 and a price of 100 before tax, the profit is 40: a 40% margin and a 66.67% markup. The calculators in this category always work with amounts before tax and tell you which base each percentage uses.

To work with the tax on an invoice, see the {{category.title:facturacion}} category.

Discounts: percentages are multiplied

Two successive discounts of 20% and 10% do not add up to 30%: the second applies to the price that is already reduced. With a price of 100 the result is 72 and the effective discount is 28%. If you need to know what markup is required to reach a given margin, the markup calculator does the conversion for you.
