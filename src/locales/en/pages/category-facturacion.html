What you can do in this category

The {{tool.title:iva}} lets you add tax to a tax base, remove tax from a total to get the base, and break down an invoice with several lines and different tax rates. You type the percentages: the tool does not assume the rules of any country.

The tool does arithmetic with the data and rates you enter. It does not decide which tax rate applies to a transaction: for that, check the official tax information for your country or ask your adviser.

If you want to set a price and see the effect of tax on the final price, use the {{tool.title:precio}}, within {{category.title:margenes-y-precios}}.
