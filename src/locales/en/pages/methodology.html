What the calculators do

Each calculator applies arithmetic formulas to the data you enter and shows the result together with the formulas and assumptions used. They are mathematical estimates: they are not individual tax, accounting or legal advice.

General criteria

  • Amounts before tax. Margin, pricing and profitability calculations use amounts before tax. Sales tax is added to the price and is not part of the margin or the profit.
  • Margin and markup. Margin on sales is measured on the selling price; markup on cost is measured on the cost. They are different percentages for the same profit.
  • Base of the percentages. In the gross and net margin calculator, every percentage is calculated on net revenue (sales minus returns and discounts).
  • Tax. The calculators do not estimate tax due. Where they accept an income tax figure, it is an amount you enter yourself. The tax-related calculators apply the percentage you type and know nothing about the rates or rules of any country.

Rounding

Results are shown with two decimals, but internal calculations are not rounded. The exception is the {{tool.title:iva}}: it works in the smallest currency unit, so the base and the tax are rounded to the nearest unit and base + tax always equals the total. In several-lines mode, the base of each line is rounded and the tax is calculated on the total base of each rate.

Data privacy

Calculations run in your browser. The calculators do not send the amounts you enter to any server.

How we review the content

The explanations include examples with concrete figures that you can check by hand, and the calculators’ formulas are checked by automated tests against those examples. Tax-related information (rates, withholdings, income tax) needs validation with official sources before the site is launched. The English text is a translation that has not yet been reviewed by a native speaker.

Limits

  • The calculators do not determine which tax rate applies to each transaction.
  • They do not include tax withheld on invoices and do not estimate income tax.
  • They do not validate how you classify each cost or expense item or its tax treatment.
  • For your tax obligations, check official sources from your country’s tax authority or ask your adviser.
